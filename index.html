Maya Studio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Maya Studio is a private tool used only by its owner to publish short fitness videos to the owner's own Facebook Page ("Maya Fitness Coach") and Instagram account (@maya.fitnesscoach.ai). "Maya" is an AI-generated character. Maya Studio is not offered to the public and no one else can log in to it.

What information we access

Through the Meta Graph API, Maya Studio accesses only the owner's own Facebook Page and linked Instagram professional account, in order to:

  • publish videos (Reels) and their captions;
  • read basic account and Page details (name, ID, username) needed to publish.

Maya Studio does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about other people, followers or visitors. It does not read private messages, and it does not use data for advertising.

How information is stored

Access tokens and the videos being published are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to publish content to the owner's accounts. Nothing is shared with third parties.

Data deletion

Maya Studio stores no data about anyone other than its owner. If you believe any of your information has been processed by Maya Studio, or you want it removed, email mayastudioai@gmail.com and it will be deleted within 30 days. The owner can remove Maya Studio's access at any time in Facebook Settings → Business Integrations.
